Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

NutriScan ("we", "our", or "us") is built by Nuv Ahuja, Neil Sachdev, and Aarav Chada. This Privacy Policy explains how NutriScan handles your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

NutriScan allows you to create a personal profile that may include:

  • Dietary preferences and goals
  • Food scan history
  • Nutritional preferences

All of this data is stored locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal profile data on our servers.

2. Third-Party Services

NutriScan uses the following third-party services to power its features:

  • Groq Vision API — used to analyze food images. Images may be temporarily processed by Groq's servers solely to return nutritional data. Groq does not retain your images.
  • USDA Food Data Central — a public U.S. government database used to retrieve nutritional information. No personal data is sent to USDA.

3. Camera Access

NutriScan requests access to your device's camera solely to scan food items. Photos are processed in real time and are not saved to your device's photo library or transmitted beyond the food recognition service.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties. Your profile data never leaves your device.

5. Children's Privacy

NutriScan is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
